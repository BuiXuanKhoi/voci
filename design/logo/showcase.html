Volar · Brand Identity

The Upbeat V

An executive-function assistant for ADHD professionals. One mark, one idea: a wing-stroke that lifts a single spark into the light.

◆
Concept. "Volar" means to fly. The mark is the letter V reimagined as a wing on the upbeat — a curved stroke that sweeps forward and lifts one spark off its tip into the light. That spark is Volar's promise: the one lit thing to do now, carried upward, forward. Flight + the studio spotlight, in a single geometric gesture.
